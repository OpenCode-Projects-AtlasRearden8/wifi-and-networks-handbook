# The Calm Home Network Handbook

*A plain-English manual for people who just want the internet to work.*

---

## Who this is for

This handbook is for anyone whose home network feels like a box of mystery:

- You pay for "fast" internet, but Wi-Fi still crawls.
- Someone told you to "try the 5 GHz one" and you nodded politely.
- Your internet drops, and you're not sure whether to blame the router, the provider, or Mercury retrograde.
- The router admin page looks like it was designed for aerospace engineers.

None of that is a personal failing. Home networks are genuinely confusing, and most documentation is written for people who don't need it. This manual is written the way a friend would explain it over coffee: plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## A calm note before we start

Nothing in your house is "broken" in a way you can't handle.

- Almost every home-network problem has a boring explanation and a boring fix.
- Unplugging and plugging things back in is a legitimate, professional technique. It is not cheating.
- You cannot break your router by reading its admin page. You can only "break" it by changing settings — and even then, a factory reset button fixes nearly everything.
- Your internet being slow is not a diagnosis of your character. It's physics, placement, and a few settings.

## How to use this handbook

1. **Skim chapters 1 and 2** — they explain most of why things behave the way they do.
2. **Fill out the [network map worksheet](network-map-worksheet.md)** one evening. It takes about fifteen minutes and pays off every time something goes wrong later.
3. **Fix one thing at a time.** Change one setting, test, keep it or undo it. This is the single most important habit in all of home networking.
4. **When the internet drops, don't improvise.** Open [chapter 8](08-when-the-internet-drops.md) and follow the steps in order.

You don't need to read everything. You need only enough to feel calm at the moment something goes wrong.

## What this handbook is not

- It is not medical, legal, or financial advice, and it is not for businesses with server rooms. Small Networks Only.
- It doesn't cover mesh-specific setups brand by brand; the concepts still apply.
- It assumes an ordinary house apartment setup: one internet provider, one router (yours), and devices that connect over Wi-Fi or a cable.
