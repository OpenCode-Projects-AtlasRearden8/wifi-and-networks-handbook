# Network map worksheet

Fill this out once, calmly, with a cup of tea. It has three jobs:

1. **Troubleshooting gets faster.** When the internet dies, you won't ask "what do we even have?" — you'll look at your map.
2. **Mystery devices stop being mysterious.** The admin-page device list is much easier to read when you already named everything.
3. **If you ever call support, you sound like a professional.** "I have a separate modem and router; the WAN light is dark; I've power-cycled both" is a sentence that changes your support call.

Print this page (the site has a print-friendly layout), or copy the tables into any notes app. Paper in a labeled folder by the router is the local-first champion move.

## Part 2 — The numbers you should know cold

Fill these in once; they answer most future questions.

| Item | Value | Where you find it |

## Part 4 — Placement questions (the quiet fix)

Fill these out standing up. Look at the router and be honest; nobody is judging.

- [ ] Router is in the **center** of the house? (Y/N — if it's in a corner, note whether it can move.)
- [ ] Router is **elevated** (above waist height)? (Y/N)
- [ ] There is **open air** around it (not inside a cabinet/metal/TV unit)? (Y/N)
- [ ] The **TV/fish tank/speaker/microwave** near it? Maybe move.
- [ ] The worst-room-in-the-house is: __________. It's probably either (a) on the wrong band, (b) too far, or (c) behind more walls than its neighbors' rooms. Do the two-speed test from [chapter 2](02-why-wifi-is-slow.md) and record what you find here.
